Help

Lights aren't responding

  1. Check that this phone is on your home Wi‑Fi. Turn off cellular data if you're not sure.
  2. Check that the controller box is plugged in. Its little screen should show a number like 192.168.1.42.
  3. Type that number on the Home screen under "Can't reach your lights," then tap Connect.

Blocked network access by mistake?

Open your browser's settings for this site and set Local network access to Allow, then reload this page.
